# Iteration 6 review — make the numbers harder to misinterpret, link the data we already have, replace assumptions with observations

Follows [ITERATION_5_REVIEW.md](ITERATION_5_REVIEW.md). Theme: **stop the numbers being
misread.** This pass did not chase a bigger headline — the monitored-area ESDI barely moved
(18.26 → 18.17 real-date). It reduced the ways a reader could take a figure to mean more than
it does: it fixed a coverage ratio that mixed two different universes, gave every refinery one
canonical identity, demoted a mixed-infrastructure recovery median that was masquerading as a
repair time, replaced a prose-parsed gas capacity with structured fields behind an explicitly
experimental sub-index, exposed how fragile the transmission number is, and added a
source-backed observed-effects layer. It also fixed a real scoring bug found along the way.

**Live headline numbers are generated, not quoted here:** see
[CURRENT_STATE.md](CURRENT_STATE.md), regenerated by every build and guarded by a test so no
doc can drift again (§2).

### ESDI decomposition (frozen as-of 2026-08-28: 18.57 → 18.49, −0.08 net)

Measured at a **frozen as-of** so time decay never contaminates the attribution. The near-zero
net hides two real, opposite-signed corrections:

- **Canonical registry + Tobolsk exclusion: +0.40** (18.57 → 18.97; refining 33.68 → 34.53).
  Removing a 6.85 MTPA petrochemical complex from the *fuels-refining* denominator raises the
  struck share of what remains. This is a **denominator correction, not new disruption** — no
  incident was added. Whether the uplift is legitimate rather than cherry-picking is exactly
  what the red-team was asked to judge (see below).
- **Recovery evidence + status-multiplier fix: −0.48** (18.97 → 18.49; refining 34.53 → 33.35;
  transmission held at 21.37). Seven new sourced recovery episodes let several refineries decay
  faster (Salavat's observed 7-day restart overrides the modelled horizon). The transmission
  sector did **not** move, because the same change fixed a latent bug (below).
- Everything else — coverage universe fix, recovery redesign, gas sub-index, transmission
  sensitivity, effects, discovery, the consistency check — is **presentation or a separate
  namespace and moves the headline by exactly 0.**

## What each section did

**Coverage universe (§3-5).** The old "57.4%" divided *all* energy events by an *oil-only*
305-strike benchmark — two different universes. Corrected to oil-sector strikes ÷ 305 = 47.2%.
Other sectors get honest descriptive states ("expanded but unbenchmarked", "thin", "no events"),
never a fabricated %. A per-sector coverage matrix keeps EVENT / ASSET-INVENTORY /
RECOVERY-EVIDENCE coverage as three distinct concepts.

**Canonical refinery registry (§6-10).** One `refineries_canonical.csv` (stable id + alias set)
that both the denominator and every incident resolve against, replacing display-name string
equality. `resolve()` does exact normalised-alias matching only — an uncertain name returns
`None` and never auto-merges. Tobolsk is excluded as petrochemical, not fuels refining. Linkage
completeness (27/34 refineries = 85.6% of denominator MTPA) is reported as **identity/linkage,
explicitly not disruption coverage**.

**Recovery (§11-14).** The pooled 72-day median mixed a 2-day terminal restart with a 205-day
gas-plant repair; it is demoted everywhere (ribbon + tab) to a caveated "mixed-infrastructure"
line. A per-class median appears only at n≥3 within a class (refining now qualifies: 47 d). A
granular `recovery_kind` records what a source actually proves (`flow_rerouted`,
`unit_restarted`, …), and a flow-rerouted restart can never read as facility reconstitution.
Corpus grown 13 → 20 records / 5 → 7 observed episodes, all source-verified; ~10 candidate
incidents were **omitted** for want of usable restoration reporting rather than guessed.

**Status-multiplier fix (found during §11-14).** `_weight_at` applied the incident's status
damping (`degraded` ×0.7) only when there was *no* recovery record. Attaching a partial-restart
record — which by design doesn't change the decay — silently dropped that damping and scored a
facility *higher* than with no evidence at all, which is how adding two transmission recovery
records briefly inflated transmission +6.16. Fixed so the status damping applies whenever the
recovery kind is `modelled`; a partial restart can now never raise a score. Regression-tested.

**Gas processing (§16-20).** Capacities moved from prose into explicit
`capacity_bcm_y`/`_basis`/`_status` fields. An **experimental** within-census exposure (weighted
disrupted GPP capacity ÷ 91.56 bcm/y censused) reports 26.8%, in a separate `experimental_indices`
namespace, with a caveat that it is not national exposure and is gated out of the headline. LNG /
gas-condensate (Novatek Ust-Luga) is kept separate from the processing census.

**Transmission audit (§21-23).** The formula is unchanged (Crimea's effect is not tuned away);
instead the alternatives are exposed: a saturation sweep showing the value ranges 4× (10.4 at
sat 16 → 41.8 at sat 4; 20.9 at the shipped sat 8), the breadth (3 regions / 5 facilities, top
theatre 54.5%), and per-region saturated values (Taman 11.4, Crimea 9.3). Red-team verdict
recorded: **retained** in the headline with mandatory concentration disclosure, not presented as
national grid exposure.

**Strategic effects (§25-28).** A structured, source-backed observed-effects layer
(`effects.csv`), every row an evidence-tagged consequence. 15 effects across 8 incidents + 4
macro datapoints (≈¼ of Russian refining offline as of 20 May 2026; 16/38 refineries hit; export
bans; ration cards). Region population is never an effect; no consumer count was inferred from
population (none was found in any source, and none was invented). No repair-cost dollar figure
was found, so none is stored.

**Candidate discovery (§29-30).** A best-effort, opt-in, human-gated scaffold that writes
`needs_review` pointers (headline + URL only) to `data/review/`, never the scored dataset, and
fails safely. GDELT is unreachable from this environment, so it returns nothing without error —
the honest §30 outcome: a triage aid, not an ingestion pipeline. Enforced by tests that the
build never imports it and the queue schema carries no location/score fields.

**Consistency check (§2).** `docs/CURRENT_STATE.md` is regenerated from the snapshot every
build; a test fails if it drifts. Narrative docs point there instead of hand-copying numbers.

## Independent red-team

An independent adversarial agent traced all six change areas numerically against a fresh
frozen build (2026-08-28 → ESDI 18.49). Result: **five of six CONFIRMED_OK, one real DEFECT,
three minor items — all addressed before ship.**

- **Status-multiplier fix — CONFIRMED_OK.** Traced the only four incidents it moves (Taman
  0.832→0.582, Crimea-grid 0.313→0.219, Shatura 0.041→0.029, Unecha 0→0); all monotone-down and
  justified, no double-damping, observed/estimated kinds correctly exempt.
- **Canonical registry + Tobolsk — CONFIRMED_OK.** Zero alias collisions, all 35 inventory
  refineries resolve, Tobolsk is the sole exclusion (a pure NGL cracker, correctly re-counted in
  the gas census, not double-counted). The +2.5% struck-share uplift (+0.33 ESDI) is a genuine
  correction, not cherry-picking.
- **Coverage universe — CONFIRMED_OK.** Numerator = 144 (91 refining + 53 oil-logistics), all
  kinetic strikes, zero episode double-counts.
- **Gas sub-index — CONFIRMED_OK.** Truly out of the headline (gas=0.0 in the composite), census
  is the exact structured-field sum, no bcm/y leakage into MTPA/MW denominators.
- **Transmission — mechanics CONFIRMED_OK, but a DEFECT in the surrounding representation.** The
  sweep and per-region math are self-consistent and the formula is unchanged (Crimea not tuned
  down). But the `esdi_excluded` machinery is dead code (Crimea is `esdi_included=True`), while
  two docstrings claimed occupied regions "never feed the national ESDI" — **false**: Crimea is
  ~45% of the transmission signal and +1.10 ESDI. The numbers are the intended iteration-4
  choice; the docstrings contradicted it on the most sensitive dimension.
- **Scope — CONFIRMED_OK.** No coordinates, no range-to-target, no population-as-affected; the
  only outage effect is a 90-minute duration that explicitly records "no consumer count".

**Fixes applied in response (no scoring change — frozen ESDI stays 18.49):**

1. Corrected the two false Crimea docstrings (`build_index.py`) to state plainly that occupied
   Crimea is intentionally **included** in the national ESDI via transmission/oil-logistics and
   is a large share of the transmission signal; the `esdi_excluded` set is documented as
   currently empty but retained. The transmission `red_team_verdict` now says outright that
   occupied Crimea is ~half the transmission signal folded into the "national" figure.
2. Corrected the one genuine data contradiction: Unecha's incident `status` was `repaired`
   against a record saying the pumping station was destroyed and only flow rerouted — changed to
   `degraded` (consistent with its `partial_restart`/`flow_rerouted` record). A new test
   (`test_incident_status_does_not_contradict_its_recovery_record`) prevents recurrence.
3. The refinery reconciliation note now discloses the Tobolsk exclusion's **direction** (+2.5%
   struck-share, ~+0.33 ESDI), not just its existence.

The status-multiplier's coupling to the crude `incident.status` field (rather than deriving
severity from the recovery record) is a known latent wart, now fenced by the consistency test;
a broader refactor was declined because deriving severity from `recovery_status` would silently
re-weight every live partial-restart incident, which this pass was not scoped to do.

## Known limitations (carried or new)

1. **The denominator correction lifts the headline.** Excluding Tobolsk is defensible
   (petrochemical, not fuels), but any denominator shrink raises the struck share; the
   experimental gas sub-index and the transmission sensitivity exist precisely because
   single-denominator measures are fragile here.
2. **Recovery is still thin (n=7 observed).** Transmission has recovery *evidence* (2 partial
   restarts) but no observed full-restoration duration; its class median is still unavailable.
3. **The gas sub-index is a within-census share, not national exposure**, and the census is a
   non-exhaustive bottom-up sample.
4. **Transmission remains theatre-concentrated** and sensitive to the saturation constant, now
   disclosed rather than hidden.
5. **Candidate discovery is inert here** (no reachable feed); it is a demonstrated-safe scaffold,
   not a working ingestion path.
6. **Effects are sparse by design** — only what a source states, so most incidents carry none.
