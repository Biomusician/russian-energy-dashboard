# Research coverage of the Gulf systems (iteration 19)

**SOURCE FRESHNESS IS NOT RESEARCH COMPLETENESS, AND A RUN PROCEDURE IS NOT COMPLETE KNOWLEDGE.** This
page answers one question for each of the eight Gulf systems: how was its record of events searched
for in the pass of 2026-11-11? It carries process facts only. It ranks nothing, and a row with
more lanes or more searches is not a better-known country.

*State as of 2026-11-11. Generated from the curated research logs; the country pages show the same facts lane by lane.*

## What the pass was

Seven systems were researched for the first time and Saudi Arabia was searched again on its own
plan. Lanes, their languages and their queries were declared and committed before any search
(`e825386`). Sixteen research agents worked the lanes, each with a fixed share of a limit of 200
web searches per turn that is shared by the whole session and was not worked around. Every lane
that the limit stopped says so.

All seven new systems are marked **research preview**. Few records means few found so far.

## What this pass could not do

- **Earlier years are thin.** The search tool gave little weight to year terms, so 2022 to 2024
  were reached mostly through publishers' own archives and listings, where those were readable.
- **Official archives were often unreadable.** Several ministries, regulators and national oil
  companies serve script-only pages, refuse automated access, or publish reports larger than the
  fetch tool reads. Those lanes say so; no workaround was attempted.
- **Two utilities forbid linking** in their terms of use (Bahrain's and Qatar's). They were not
  cited, which leaves both countries' electricity lanes resting on press copies of statements.
- **Citations were not link-checked** for the Gulf systems in this pass.
- **Attack records were researched for occurrence only.** Restart, repair and operating status
  were deliberately not collected for any system classified as under an active or uncertain
  campaign, which is all eight.

## Reading the published counts

Counts per system are on each country page, beside its reporting environment. They are not
comparable between systems: a country whose ministry publishes dated statements of power cuts
will show more records than one whose utility publishes nothing, whatever happened in each.
